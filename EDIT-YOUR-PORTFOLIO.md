# Edit your portfolio

Open **src/content/portfolio.ts** in Lovable's code editor. All your personal content is in that one file.

- Change the text between quotation marks beside `name`, `role`, `tagline`, `introduction`, and `about`.
- Replace the interests and skills with your own.
- Add your email, GitHub link, and LinkedIn link. Empty contact details are hidden.
- Add completed projects to `projects`. An example is included as a comment in the file. Until then, the work page honestly says projects are coming.
- Save to update the preview. Publish when you want your updates live.

The introductory bio and interests are starter copy, not verified qualifications. Replace them with your own story.

## Artwork and type

Artwork is original Rockstar Games artwork, sourced from GTABase's official artwork gallery, not AI-generated. This is an unofficial fan-inspired portfolio, not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

The distressed display font is Chinese Rocks by Typodermic, sourced via CDNFonts. Review its license and the artwork's usage rights before commercial use.
